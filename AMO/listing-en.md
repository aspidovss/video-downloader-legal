## Description
```
Save videos and audio from the page you watch — YouTube, Shorts, Instagram, Twitch, TikTok, VK, OKru, Bilibili, Dailymotion, RuTube, Dzen and more. Free, local, no paywall, no tracking.

VDL - Video Downloader Legal lists what the open page plays and saves the one you choose. Everything happens inside your browser: no external servers, no analytics, no helper programs to install. It also works in Firefox for Android; the same extension runs in Chrome, Microsoft Edge and other Chromium browsers (builds on GitHub). Video and audio are joined and TS is converted to MP4 by the extension's own code, without re-encoding.

FEATURES
- Video download — direct files, HLS (m3u8) and DASH (mpd) streams on any site: choose the quality, separate audio tracks are joined into one file.
- YouTube and YouTube Shorts — choose type (MP4, WEBM, AV1) and resolution; audio as MP3, M4A or WEBM; video and audio in one file.
- YouTube subtitles — as an .srt file or with the video.
- Ready for Telegram — video and sound of different formats are joined into an ordinary MP4 (no re-encoding), so Telegram sends it as a video.
- Clear reasons — when YouTube does not give a video, the window says why (age restriction, private, removed, region, members only, paid…) and whether recording can help. Creative Commons videos get a CC BY badge.
- Live streams — record Twitch and other HLS live streams until you press Stop; YouTube live recording from the player (beta).
- TS to MP4 — streams in MPEG-TS are saved as ordinary MP4 by default (no re-encoding); TS stays available as the second choice.
- TikTok — the video on screen is first, with its title and cover; the rest of the feed is under "More".
- Other sites — Vimeo, Dailymotion, RuTube, Dzen, Instagram, Facebook, X (Twitter), Bilibili, Canva, OKru, VK and any other site through general stream detection (results depend on what the page provides).
- Audio download — MP3 (converted in the browser), M4A or WEBM.
- Light and dark theme — follows your system. 10 languages: English, Russian, Chinese, Spanish, Hindi, French, Arabic, Portuguese, German, Japanese.
- Convenient — download history, file name template, notifications, configurable hotkeys, built-in help.
- Diagnostics — one button saves a local file (links masked) you can attach to a bug report; an optional debug log (off by default) records what the extension does. Nothing is ever sent automatically.

PRIVACY FIRST
No accounts, no telemetry, no servers of our own. Settings and history stay in your browser. The extension contacts only the sites you are already using.

LEGAL BY DESIGN
DRM-protected streams are detected and refused, never decrypted. No tokens, signatures or sessions are forged. Please save only what you have the right to save.

OPEN SOURCE (MIT)
The only third-party file is the MP3 encoder lamejs 1.2.1 (LGPL-3.0), a JavaScript port of LAME (https://lame.sourceforge.net/). It is built from the unmodified lamejs sources by a script included in the source code, so anyone can rebuild and check it.

AI DISCLOSURE
This project contains code generated by AI. All texts, comments and descriptions are AI-generated. All translations are AI-generated.

GOOD TO KNOW
- After installing, allow access to all sites in about:addons → the extension → Permissions, otherwise the page scanner cannot see the media.
- Streaming sites change often; YouTube and social networks are supported on a best-effort basis and may stop working later. If something does not work, press the bug button in the window and send the diagnostics file.
- DRM-protected services (Netflix, Disney+ and similar) are not supported and never will be.
```
